MORROW — CURRENT DESIGN DECISIONS
24 November 2026 · draft reconciliation of direct conversation decisions

FOUNDATION
DM Sans throughout; balanced neutral workspace; white desktop sidebar separated
by a hairline; white working surfaces; charcoal text; selective acid lime.
No final logo has been approved. Do not invent logos, workspace switchers,
top toolbars, notification systems, badges, roles or paid-account features.

COLOURS
Canvas #F5F5F7; surfaces/sidebar #FFFFFF; text #121316; secondary #5E636E;
border #E5E6EB; neutral controls #ECECEF; primary lime #D4FC30 with charcoal text;
desktop selected navigation #F2FCD6; mobile nav #121316; inner selected #2F3132;
error/danger #BA1A1A; disabled #8E94A0; focus olive #536600.
Olive is for focus rings, not primary button fills. Disabled grey is not a
general body/placeholder colour. Avoid generated green/blue-tinted themes.

ACTION HIERARCHY — LATEST USER CORRECTION
Enabled primary submission actions use lime. Do not retain charcoal primary
buttons from older dialogs or save screens. Neutral controls open supporting
flows or cancel them. Disabled controls are grey. The global New idea trigger
is neutral. Add example is secondary on the Voice page, but primary inside its
editor. Sign out is red text + logout icon on a transparent/quiet surface.

SHAPES AND TYPE
Capture 20px; working surfaces/popovers 16px; standard controls 12px; compact
controls 8px. Full circles for avatars/send. Floating nav is a capsule.
Explicit exception: Add context is a filled neutral pill, not outlined.
Settings page heading 24/32 semibold; section labels 15/20 medium (not 18px);
mobile input/textarea text 16px; helpers typically 13/18. Use approved screen
references for creative hero typography; do not apply hero sizes to settings.
Mobile side padding 24px, typical page top padding 64px, >=44px touch targets.
These do not force Home's centred desktop composition into a list-page layout.

SURFACES
Browsing lists are flat with spacing/dividers. Fields have no drop shadows.
A pending learning suggestion is one bounded white working surface with 16px
corners; its evidence and decision buttons belong inside that surface.
Use temporary elevation for menus/dialogs/sheets; avoid cards inside cards.

SHARED SIDEBAR
240px wide. White. Neutral New idea at top; Home, Studio, Library, Ideas, Brand
Brain; Calendar and You at bottom; divider then red Sign out below You.
Use component_desktop_sidebar_you_active's sidebar region; the empty canvas
to its right is not part of the component. Apply this updated footer across
desktop screens and remove the old duplicate Sign out in Account's body.
Changing the current route changes only the selected destination treatment.

MOBILE NAV
Home / Studio / Library / Ideas / Brand Brain. Shared SVG assets; icons only
except selected destination's icon + label. Brain uses the preserved exact asset at
`artifacts/phase-2/references/brain.svg` (also rendered by the shared BrainIcon).
The user explicitly requested a selected Brain pill on the mobile You screens.
Carry this visual exception; do not silently add a sixth destination or change
its appearance back to unselected. Avoid falsely exposing aria-current=page
for the Brain route when the real route is You. This is a known IA exception.
Global nav is visible on Studio entrance but hidden in active Write/Discuss.
Sheets/dialogs cover navigation and make background controls inactive.

PROFILE HEADER — LATEST DECISION, NOT SHALLOW OVERLAP
You owns personal profile/account settings. Brand Brain owns writing context.
Cover is a replaceable image slot with mint/peach gradient fallback; avatar is
a personal image slot with initials fallback, not a paper illustration.
Gravatar was mentioned as a possibility, not approved as an integration.

Desktop: cover 160px high; avatar 128px, white rim 4px, left inset 24px.
Mobile: cover 112px; avatar 96px, white rim 4px, left inset 12px.
Avatar vertical centre MUST coincide with cover bottom: 50% above, 50% below.
Anchor avatar to a cover-only relative wrapper. The rounded gradient is an
inner clipped surface; the avatar's positioning wrapper must not clip it.
Do not anchor top:100% to a wrapper that also contains name/footer content.

Desktop name block left=176px from cover edge; top=12px below cover bottom.
Name 20/28 semibold; common helper 13/20 with 4px gap; reserve 64px lower-header
height, then 32px before tabs. Mobile left=124px, top=8px below cover; name18/24,
helper13/18 with4px gap; reserve natural height for wrap and lower half of avatar,
then24px before tabs. Common helper: Your profile and account settings.
Camera affordances may look compact but must have >=44px hit areas.
Profile and Account must use the SAME desktop header and content width.
Account status values are right-aligned beside buttons: value220px, gap24px,
button160px x40px on desktop; adapt without clipping on smaller screens.

BRAND BRAIN
Four tabs: Identity, Voice, Content strategy, Learning & feedback.
On mobile the tab strip scrolls horizontally; active label fully visible after
fonts/layout settle. No tiny text to force all labels into one viewport.
Learning uses To review and Approved preferences, with counts; hide empty
Patterns & examples. One short review-policy sentence; no repeated helper wall.
Pending suggestion's main statement is prominent; evidence starts collapsed
under Why this suggestion?; Approve lime and Dismiss neutral. Approved items
are flat entries with separate Edit/Remove row. No page Save in Learning.

ILLUSTRATIONS
Approved family: fine controlled outlines, white paper, muted mint/peach patches,
gentle depth inside artwork. Use intentionally for Studio secondary entry routes
and first-use Ideas/Library empty states. Do not illustrate populated list rows.
CSS/SVG stand-ins are acceptable references. This ZIP is not a final standalone
illustration asset kit. Refine/export assets during implementation without
changing the approved family. Profile photos are separate from this system.

MOTION
Purposeful state transitions; respect reduced motion. Arc/Motion may now support adopted UIArc components. Specific application
animations, shader effects and decorative motion still require design justification.
The cover fallback does not imply a GPU shader or animated background.

FIRST-BUILD SCOPE OVERRIDE — 24 NOVEMBER
The complete sidebar reference contains Calendar. The user has now deferred
Calendar and manual publication recording; hide Calendar in the live first-build
navigation rather than implementing a dead route. Preserve the future reference
asset. Use the same remaining sidebar anatomy, You selection and red Sign out.

UIARC ADOPTION — 24 NOVEMBER
User choice: add UIArc components alongside shadcn/ui. The approved Morrow theme,
geometry, icons and behaviour stay authoritative. Arc CSS modules and selected
Motion-based interactions are allowed through one shared component/theme layer.
Read UIARC-INTEGRATION.txt; do not import Arc's stock fonts, radii or whole-page
layouts as a new design direction. No dark-mode redesign or application AI
provider is selected by this component-library decision.

HOME REDESIGN — 26 NOVEMBER 2026
Deliberate Home updates supersede older Stitch Home export details where they
conflict. Preserve light neutral canvas, white surfaces, DM Sans, lime primary
actions, existing line icons and the heading “Make something of today.”

Composer: compact initial field (~two visible lines; textarea min ~56px, max
~160px). Grow/shrink with text using the installed Motion system (~120–160ms,
no bounce); at max height the field scrolls and Save stays reachable. Separate
the footer with spacing only — no horizontal rule. No added focus frame, ring
or shadow on the textarea or enclosing composer; Save and other controls keep
normal keyboard focus. Preserve shortcuts, validation, save and failure recovery.

Action cards: Continue, Revisit an idea and Blank draft share one compact
composition with equal heights in each row (three equal columns on wide Home;
readable single column on narrow mobile). Keep the Continue label even when
empty — do not rename it to “Recent writing”. One recent record per relevant
card; preview text clamps to two lines. Move “View all ideas” (and Continue’s
“See all”) outside the card group as secondary links so they cannot stretch one
card. Empty Continue / Revisit stay non-actionable (no hover/press affordance).

Card feedback: resting white + subtle border; hover muted surface + slightly
stronger border; optional ≤1px lift on fine-pointer hover; press scale ~0.985
via Motion; visible keyboard focus; respect reduced motion. No persistent
selected state, heavy shadows, bounce, decorative tilt or looping animation.
Do not add 3D artwork or new illustrations in this pass.
